## 2. What we collect and why

| Information | Where | Why | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voice | On the phone only* | Turn speech into text | Never recorded by Vocourse |
| Dictated text | Phone; Vocourse server while analyzing | Recognize the items | Not stored by the server (technical response kept 48 h, encrypted, to prevent duplicates) |
| Current list | Phone (encrypted storage) | Nothing gets lost | Until sent, 12 months max |
| Sent lists and confirmations (text read back, time of both confirmations) | Vocourse server (encrypted) | Proof of consent to the order, right of access | 12 months, then deleted automatically |
| First names (person helped — optional — and caregiver), caregiver's cell number, delivery address, preferred store, language | Phone and server (encrypted) | Notify the caregiver, prepare the Instacart list | Until the account is deleted, or 12 months without use |
| Caregiver code | Phone only, hashed | Protect the caregiver area | Until changed |
| Technical device identifier | Phone and server | Secure communications | Until the account is deleted |

\* If the phone can't recognize speech offline, and **only if the caregiver allowed it**
during setup, audio is sent to Apple's or Google's speech recognition service, depending
on the phone, under their own policies.

No location, contacts or advertising ID. No advertising, profiling, sale or rental of data.

**Sensitive items** (e.g. incontinence products) are flagged: never named in texts and
never given automatic filters.

## 3. Sharing

| Recipient | What | Why | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instacart (Maplebear Inc.) | List items (name, quantity), dated title, name of the preferred store — **no** name, address or number | Create the list page where the caregiver picks the store and pays | [United States / Canada — to confirm] |
| Instacart (Maplebear Inc.) | Delivery postal code only (no street address or identity), when the caregiver looks up nearby stores | Suggest stores that deliver to that address | [United States / Canada — to confirm] |

| Host [name] | All server data, encrypted | Host the service | [Montreal, Quebec] |
| Apple / Google (optional) | Dictation audio | Online speech recognition, if allowed | [per provider] |

Payment is made by the caregiver on Instacart: Vocourse receives no payment data.

## 4. Consent
Consent is requested at first use by the caregiver, then separately for texts and for online
speech recognition. Withdraw at any time: reply **STOP** to a Vocourse text (START to resume),
or *Caregiver area → Privacy → Delete the account and data*.

## 5. Security
HTTPS, encryption at rest on the server (AES-256-GCM) and on the phone (Keychain/Keystore),
caregiver area protected by a 6-digit code (locked after 3 attempts, caregiver alerted),
technical logs without personal information, restricted server access [to complete].

## 6. Your rights
Access, correction, deletion and portability (structured, commonly used format). The
caregiver can do this in the app (*Export my data*, *Delete the account*) or by writing to
the privacy officer. Answer within 30 days. Complaints: Office of the Privacy Commissioner
of Canada (www.priv.gc.ca), your provincial commissioner, or in Quebec the Commission
d'accès à l'information (www.cai.gouv.qc.ca).

## 7. Incidents
We keep a register of privacy incidents and notify the regulator and affected people when
there is a risk of serious harm.

## 8. Children
Vocourse is not intended for people under 14.

## 9. Changes
Material changes will be announced in the app and, if needed, require new consent.
